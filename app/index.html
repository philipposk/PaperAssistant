A tidy workspace for your research paper

PaperAssistant keeps the pieces of a research paper in one place: create a project, add your figures, tables and documents, write notes beside them, and browse everything in a clean layout.

What it does

  • Separate projects for each paper
  • Upload your own figures, tables and documents
  • Keep notes next to your materials
  • Read everything in a clean, focused layout
  • Works offline in your browser
  • Sign in to sync a copy to the cloud across devices
